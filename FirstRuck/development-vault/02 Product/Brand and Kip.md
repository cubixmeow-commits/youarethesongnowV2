# Brand and Kip

## Visual direction

FirstRuck should feel like a premium outdoor field guide brought to life:

- Forest ink: `#14331d`
- Warm field paper: `#f9f3e6`
- Safety orange action: `#f45707`
- Editorial Georgia-style serif headlines with restrained sans-serif interface text
- Real outdoor photography, topographic line motifs, generous space, and one dominant action
- Avoid generic AI-dashboard cards, excessive gradients, glowing effects, and fake data density

The complete brand baseline and original assets live in `brand/`. The current experience styles live in `experience-lab/app.css`.

## Kip

Kip is a friendly wombat with taupe gouache fur, a broad nose, and an orange backpack with green straps. Kip represents steady progress, preparation, curiosity, and companionship.

Use Kip at introductions, teaching breaks, completion, empty journal, and Journey moments. Keep Kip out of the active map area during recording. Never make Kip disappointed after missed days. Never reward heavier weight, calorie burn, or streaks through Kip.

Voice examples:

- “I packed enthusiasm. And probably too many snacks.”
- “We’re collecting good walks. Not heroic regrets.”
- “If your backpack has a drum solo, repack.”

Character humor must stay gentle and situational. No jokes in symptom, safety, privacy, payment-error, or data-loss messages.

## Asset rules

- Current selected mascot asset: `experience-lab/assets/kip-wombat-cutout.png`.
- Keep the original prompt and edit history in `experience-lab/assets/IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`.
- Maintain consistent anatomy, pack color, straps, painterly texture, and scale in future poses.
- Crop photography around the activity being taught. The pack lesson specifically focuses on face, hands, straps, and chest buckle.
- Use the route logo as the product mark; Kip is the companion, not a replacement logo.
- Provide meaningful alt text for instructional images and empty alt text for purely decorative marks.
