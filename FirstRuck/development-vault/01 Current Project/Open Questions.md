# Open questions

These decisions are unresolved. Do not silently answer them in implementation.

## Product and membership

- What is free, what requires membership, and when does entitlement begin?
- What are the real monthly and annual products, trial rules, and launch regions?
- Is the first prepared route usable before purchase, or only previewed?
- What is the promised cadence and logic of plan adaptation?
- Which outcomes justify the subscription after the first month?

## Route quality

- Which evidence is mandatory before calling a route beginner-suitable?
- How should urban sidewalk routes, greenways, parks, and trails be distinguished?
- Which providers will supply elevation, surface, access, closures, crossings, and weather?
- What should happen when the requested duration cannot produce a credible loop?
- How will the team test routes outside its local area?

## Coaching and safety

- Which during-walk messages are fixed rules, and which may use an LLM?
- Who reviews training progression and symptom guidance?
- What events should pause or end a recording automatically, if any?
- How should poor GPS accuracy and battery use be explained?

## Identity, privacy, and social

- Is an account optional until purchase or sync?
- What is stored locally, on the server, or in object storage?
- How long are tracks and photos retained, and how can users export or delete them?
- Is social sharing external-only first, or will there be an in-app feed?
- If community is built, who can see posts and how are moderation and location redaction handled?

## Platform

- Which web browsers are supported for recording and native sharing?
- When should Flutter replace the web reference for product decisions?
- Is background tracking required for the first native release?
- Will Android ship with iOS or later?
