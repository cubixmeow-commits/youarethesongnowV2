# First Ruck image-generation prompts

Shared direction: premium civilian outdoor editorial photography; attainable beginner effort; realistic pack fit and anatomy; natural skin and body types; forest, charcoal, warm paper, and restrained safety-orange detail; no text, logos, watermark, tactical styling, oversized load, summit theater, or testimonial context.

## Hero beginner greenway

Create a portrait onboarding hero of a relatable adult woman beginning a calm walk on an accessible neighborhood greenway at dawn. She wears practical layered walking clothes, supportive shoes, and a modest well-fitted olive daypack. Place her right of center with dark-green negative space at upper-left and below for UI. Use cool dawn light with a warm rim and premium editorial realism.

## Community park walk

Create a wide premium photograph of three diverse adults around ages 30, 45, and 58 walking together on an urban park loop with modest backpacks and relaxed conversational effort. Use natural body types, practical unmatched clothing, low warm sunlight, and documentary warmth. Keep the group in the right two-thirds and leave quiet space left/top.

## Equipment flat-lay

Create a vertical premium flat-lay on warm recycled paper with faint topographic embossing: an unbranded sturdy olive daypack open to reveal two water bottles cushioned in a folded towel, comfortable walking shoes, wool-blend socks, a small blister bandage, a phone, and a drinking bottle. Leave upper-left negative space.

## Pack-fit adjustment

Create a vertical outdoor instruction photograph of a relatable man around age 48 in profile at a park trailhead, correctly adjusting the sternum strap of a compact backpack worn high and close. Keep torso, pack, and hands readable; place him on the right with calm negative space at left.

## Route choice greenway

Create a wide environmental photograph of a public neighborhood greenway where a paved route meets a gentle compacted park path, with benches and a water fountain in the distance. An adult woman with a small backpack walks away at an easy pace; the route is the hero. Use calm morning light and a path curving from lower-left toward center-right.

## Completion portrait

Create a vertical premium portrait of an approachable East Asian man in his late 20s after a short neighborhood ruck, wearing an ordinary fitted daypack and practical walking clothes. Show relaxed upright posture, a subtle natural smile, and slight realistic perspiration. Place him left with warm quiet space at right for plan copy.
